# Mentoring and Social Adaptation Platform

## Project Overview

The Mentoring and Social Adaptation Platform is a web-based project designed to support children living in orphanages and residential care institutions, as well as graduates of boarding schools transitioning to independent living.

The platform aims to facilitate access to mentorship, guidance, educational resources, and social support. It is intended to help users build meaningful connections with mentors and develop skills that support their social adaptation and independent living.

## Problem Statement

Children growing up in residential care and graduates transitioning to independent living may face challenges related to social integration, access to guidance, and the development of independent living skills. Access to consistent mentorship and relevant support resources can help address these challenges.

This project aims to develop a centralized digital platform that connects users with mentors and provides access to relevant resources and communication tools.

## Project Aim

To develop a web-based mentoring and social adaptation platform that facilitates mentor–mentee connections and provides resources to support social integration and independent living.

## Target Users

- **Children in residential care:** Young people who may benefit from mentorship and educational resources.
- **Boarding school graduates:** Young adults transitioning to independent living.
- **Mentors:** Individuals who provide guidance, share experience, and support mentees.
- **Administrators:** Users responsible for managing platform accounts and overseeing platform activities.

## Planned Features

The following features are planned for development:

- User registration and authentication.
- Role-based access control for different user types.
- Mentor profiles and mentor–mentee matching.
- Communication between mentors and mentees.
- Access to educational and social adaptation resources.
- Notifications and updates.
- Administrative tools for managing platform activities.
