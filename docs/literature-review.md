# 3. Literature Review

## 3.1 Critical Review

Existing research on mentoring and social adaptation uses three main approaches: empirical studies of care leavers, digital mentoring platforms, and computational matching algorithms.

Studies by Thompson and Greeson (2017), Sadyrova and Simtikov (2025), and Kaasinen et al. (2025) highlight the importance of social connections, individualized support, and preparation for independent living. Research by Radlick et al. (2020) also identifies secure communication, goal tracking, and practical resources as important digital mentoring features.

Technical studies by Haas et al. (2018), Varghese and Mohaghegh (2023), and Kleinerman et al. (2021) explore preference-based matching and recommendation algorithms. However, their approaches have limitations, including small datasets or application to domains other than youth mentoring.

Overall, existing research supports combining structured mentoring, life-skills development, secure communication, and compatibility-based recommendations. However, limited research directly addresses digital mentoring platforms for orphanage residents and care leavers in Kazakhstan. This project addresses this gap through a web platform with AI-assisted mentor recommendations, coordinator supervision, and personalized social adaptation goals.

## 3.3 Research Gap and Project Contribution

The reviewed literature demonstrates the value of mentoring, social adaptation support, and preference-based matching. However, technical matching studies often use small datasets or settings unrelated to care leavers, while studies of vulnerable youth rarely evaluate AI-based digital platforms.

The proposed platform combines these areas by providing AI-assisted mentor recommendations based on interests, needs, location, and availability. Coordinators retain final matching authority, while goal tracking, secure communication, and progress monitoring support young people transitioning toward independent living. The system will be designed for the Kazakhstan context.
